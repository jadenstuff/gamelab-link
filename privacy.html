The Game Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026

The Game Lab sells Roblox game templates through its Discord server. This policy explains what our Discord bot stores and why.

What we collect

  • Discord account ID and username — to open your private purchase ticket and deliver your order.
  • Roblox user ID — only if you choose to link your Roblox account for Robux checkout. We request the openid and profile scopes and use your Roblox user ID to check whether your account owns the game pass you bought.
  • Order records — product, payment method, price, invoice amount, payment reference or blockchain transaction ID, and order status.
  • Reviews and suggestions you choose to submit.

What we do not collect

We do not store Roblox passwords, cookies or OAuth access tokens. The access token is used once to read your Roblox user ID and is then discarded. We do not collect payment card details.

How we use it

Only to run the store: verify payments, deliver files, prevent the same payment or game pass from being redeemed twice, and post reviews you submit. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Where it is stored

On our bot's private database hosted by Cloudflare. Ownership checks use Roblox's public inventory APIs. Crypto payments are checked on public blockchain services.

How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept to prevent duplicate redemptions and fraud. You can ask staff in our Discord server to unlink your Roblox account or delete your other data; records needed to prevent payment fraud may be kept.

Contact

Open an inquiry in The Game Lab Discord server.
